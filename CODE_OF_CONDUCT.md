**Copyright © 2026 Sohan Ananthula ("mr.perfect"). All Rights Reserved.**

This project is **proprietary and confidential**. Access is granted only to
authorized collaborators and only for the specific purpose approved in writing
by the Owner. By accessing, cloning, or contributing to this repository, you
agree to the terms below in addition to the terms of `LICENSE.txt`.

## 1. Expected Conduct

All collaborators are expected to:

- Treat all source code, designs, assets, and discussions as **confidential**.
- Use repository access solely for authorized work on this project.
- Act professionally and respectfully toward other collaborators.
- Report any suspected leak, breach, or unauthorized access to the Owner
  immediately.

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Conduct

The following are **prohibited** and constitute a material breach of this
agreement and of `LICENSE.txt`:

- **Reverse-engineering** — decompiling, disassembling, or reverse-engineering
  any part of the codebase, build output, or design system, except where such
  a restriction is expressly forbidden by applicable law.
- **Sharing inner components** — disclosing, posting, or transmitting source
  files, components, internal APIs, design tokens, or architecture details to
  anyone outside the authorized collaborator group.
- **Staging / preview link leakage** — sharing, publishing, or exposing
  staging URLs, preview deployments, credentials, environment variables, or
  any non-public endpoint.
- **Unauthorized design forks** — creating forks, clones, derivative UIs, or
  copies of the interface or design system without the Owner's prior written
  approval.
- **Unauthorized capture** — screenshotting or recording the interface for
  redistribution, or submitting any part of the Work to generative-AI or
  UI-cloning tools.
- Committing secrets (API keys, tokens, passwords, `.env` values) to the
  repository.

## 3. Contribution Rules

- Contributions may only be made via branches and pull requests you are
  explicitly authorized to open.
- Do not add third-party dependencies without approval.
- Do not remove, alter, or obscure copyright notices, license headers, or the
  footer legal notice.
- All contributions become the exclusive property of the Owner.

## 4. Enforcement

Violations may result in **immediate revocation of access**, removal from the
project, and **legal action**, including claims for injunctive relief and
damages and the issuance of **DMCA takedown notices** to relevant hosts and
platforms. The Owner may take any of these actions at their sole discretion.
